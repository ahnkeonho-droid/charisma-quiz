What is a Charisma Style?

Charisma is the way your personality makes an impression on others. It can appear through your humor, energy, confidence, warmth, expressiveness, or quiet intensity.

🌻

Why Does Knowing It Matter?

Understanding your social style can help you recognize your strengths, communicate more confidently, and appreciate the unique qualities you bring into a room.

🪞

How Does It Work?

Answer 21 everyday social situations based on what you would naturally do. Your answers will determine which of six charisma archetypes best matches your responses.

This is an entertainment and self-reflection quiz, not a scientifically validated personality assessment.
